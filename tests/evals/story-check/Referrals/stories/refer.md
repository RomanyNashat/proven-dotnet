# Refer a patient to a specialist clinic

As a treating doctor, I want to refer my patient to a specialist clinic, so that the clinic can see them.

- AC1: A doctor refers a patient they treat to a clinic, with a reason for the referral.
- AC2: The clinic sees the new referral in its queue.
- AC3: The patient gets a text message naming the clinic and saying the clinic will call them.

## Notes from the refinement
- Paper referrals from before go-live are not moved into the system; clinics keep working them on paper.
- The text message goes out in the language set in the patient's profile.
