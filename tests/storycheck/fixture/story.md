# Cancel a booked visit

As a patient, I want to cancel a visit I booked, so that the slot goes to someone else.

- AC1: A patient can cancel their own booked visit up to 2 hours before it starts.
- AC2: A cancelled visit frees its slot at once.
- AC3: Cancelling a visit that is already cancelled changes nothing and returns the visit as it is.
- AC4: The confirmation text is shown in Arabic or English, by the app's language.
